Welcome to Our Club

The RP Tumba Tech Club is a student-led initiative that brings together passionate learners in technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship. We run project-based workshops, mentorship circles, and outreach programs to help members build practical skills and portfolios.

Our motto is: "Learning by Building". Members work in teams to design, prototype and launch projects — from websites to simple robots — while sharing knowledge with peers.
