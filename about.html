Who We Are

The RP Tumba Tech Club was founded in 2022 by a group of students who believed that technology should be accessible to everyone. Since then, we have grown into a vibrant community of innovators, coders, and problem-solvers.

"Innovation is not about tools, it’s about people who dare to think differently."

Our members collaborate on projects ranging from web development and robotics to community-based digital literacy programs. We believe in learning by doing, and every semester we launch new initiatives that challenge us to grow.

Our Mission

To empower students with
practical skills in technology, foster creativity,
and build solutions that positively impact our community.

Our Vision

A future where every student at RP Tumba has the confidence and ability to use technology to solve real-world problems.

😎Did You Know❓

The club has hosted 3 hackathons and trained over 200 students in coding and digital skills.
