What We Offer

Our club provides a variety of opportunities for students to learn, collaborate, and grow.

Club Services

  • Weekly coding workshops
  • Guest lectures from industry experts
  • Community outreach programs
  • Hackathons and competitions
  • Peer-to-peer mentoring

Steps to Join

  1. Fill out the membership form
  2. Attend an orientation session
  3. Participate in your first workshop
  4. Choose a project team
  5. Contribute regularly to club activities
